# GN-008 — Next-step ordering follows the gate, not the domain

**Harvested from:** Session 1, post-Phase-4 review — the elder-parent
test light. The emission ranked the consent conversation with the
mother as step 1, above every technology choice. Confirmed by the
co-creator as harvest note number 2.

**The law:** Recommended next steps must be ordered by what actually
gates progress toward the fundamental outcome — not by what falls
inside the lens's domain. The first next step may legitimately lie
entirely outside the domain: a conversation, a permission, a decision,
a human commitment.

**Why it matters:** a verdict lens that only sequences in-domain next
steps fails the human. It hands them step 2 when step 1 is not theirs
to skip. In the elder-parent case, no sensing technology matters until
consent exists — ordering technology first would have optimized the
wrong bottleneck and quietly disrespected the person at the center of
the ask.

**The principle:** the lens's focus governs *what it judges*; the
human's reality governs *what they must do first*. These are not the
same axis, and the emission must keep them honest to each other.

**Relation to GN-007:** sibling law. GN-007 governs how the verdict
*tilts* (honor the door they entered); GN-008 governs how the exit is
*ordered* (honor the ground they stand on). Tilt respects the entry
point; ordering respects the bottleneck.

**Reusable move:** before emitting next steps, ask: *what must be true
before any in-domain step becomes possible?* If the answer is a
non-domain human action — a conversation, a permission, a decision —
that action is step 1, and the in-domain steps follow it.

**Recommendation surface:** any lens that emits next steps, routing, or
action recommendations — effectively every lens.
