# Grind Notes

Harvested craft knowledge from lens-grinding sessions. These are
RECOMMENDED to future sessions, never injected — the co-creator opts in.

## GN-001 — Abstraction-level collapse is the grinder's first failure mode

**Harvested from:** Session 1 (the "what can AI do for me" generalized
case), while grinding the workshop's very first lens.

**What happened:** The co-creator brought a foggy one-liner ("I want to
use AI to do something for me... I'm not sure it can... I don't know how
to set it up") as the *reception sample* for a new lens. The grinder
immediately began running that sentence through an imagined lens —
decomposing the co-creator's uncertainty, hypothesizing a "Delegation
Lens," asking what the emission should contain for *them*. The grinder
became a runner. The abstraction collapsed.

**The principle:** In a grind session there are two levels, and every
question must be checked against them:

- **Level M (machine):** questions about the lens being created — its
  name, its reception condition, its questioning style, its output
  shape, its gates.
- **Level R (runner):** questions the finished lens would ask its
  eventual users.

A foggy sentence brought to the workshop is Level-M material: a sample
of the *scattering the lens must accept*. It is not to be refracted.
The moment the grinder starts answering the user's fog instead of asking
what glass should exist to receive it, the session has drifted to Level
R and must be corrected back.

**Reusable move:** When the co-creator's sample looks like a request for
help, ask: *"Am I about to run this, or grind it?"* Then stay in grind.

**Recommendation surface:** Every future grind session, Phase 0. This is
the first thing to check, because it is the first thing to fail.
